The Books

One hexagram, many centuries. Pick a place in the matrix; the rail chooses which books answer. Read to know yourself, not to be told your fate — relate to the hexagram, never obey it.

Rows: lower trigram · Columns: upper trigram — the space's native coordinates.

Data: this repo's book-grammars (public-domain texts; see each grammar's _grammar_commons). Casting a reading — the path from one hexagram to the next — lives in the Caster. Why this matrix has 8 rows and 8 columns — one bit, then three, then six, and then the same 64 bent onto a circle — is walked in the Binary Ladder. Architecture: TIME-AND-SPACE.md. English, where a book has it, is James Legge's (Sacred Books of the East vol. XVI, 1882/1899) — public domain, and a Victorian Scot's reading, not a neutral window.
