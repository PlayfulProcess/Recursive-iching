The Recursive I Ching

A historical, editorial account of the Book of Changes — how a Western Zhou diviner's manual acquired a philosophy, a canon, and a world readership. Read to know yourself, not to be told your fate — relate to the hexagram, never obey it.

The viewers

The Books

One hexagram, many centuries. An 8×8 trigram matrix, a rail of books, and the original texts side by side.

The Caster

The cast as a path: three coins or the yarrow distribution, 本卦 → 之卦, and the line texts the moving lines actually point at.

The Binary Ladder

One bit, then three, then six, then sixty-four arcs of a circle — the Leibniz/Bouvet history, and the Human Design mandala as a fourth rung.

What the data is

Four grammars, all open: the Zhouyi core text (judgment and line statements in the original language), the Ten Wings, and two structural readings of the same 64 objects — Emergent Structure (lines → trigrams → hexagrams) and the Leibniz Binary Tree of Change. They live in grammars/ and are what the viewers above read.

Public domain in, public domain out. The Zhouyi is ancient and free everywhere; the Wilhelm–Baynes English is not public domain and is deliberately absent until it is. Every picture has had its Wikimedia Commons file page opened and its licence tag read, and is registered in docs/IMAGES.md — including the candidates rejected for being CC-licensed rather than free.
